3D models (assets/models/, assets/hd/) and PBR ground materials (assets/mat/)
come from tevy (https://tevy.dev). Every tevy asset is released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Files are the unmodified tevy downloads (meshopt geometry, KTX2 textures).
  Trees:   european-beech, sugar-maple, silver-birch, grey-alder, norway-spruce
           https://tevy.dev/models/<name>
  People:  casual-walker, dog-walker, hiker, cyclist (rigged, idle/walk/wave/look_around)
  Ride:    coaster-train, coaster-loading-station
  Park:    park-bench, street-lamp, waste-bin, clipped-hedge-straight
  Plants:  red-rose-bush, marigold-edging-row, lavender-clump, tulip-cluster, blue-hydrangea
  Rocks:   granite-boulder, limestone-boulder-medium
  Terrain: mountain-ridge
  Materials: meadow-ground, gravel-shoulder, worn-concrete
           https://tevy.dev/materials/<name>

The KTX2 transcoder in js/basis/ is part of three.js (Basis Universal,
Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal).
Grass blades, steel track, supports and the station sign are generated in code.
